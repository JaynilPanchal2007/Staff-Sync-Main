# StaffSync

### Intelligent Workforce Adjustment Platform

> **When staff availability changes, StaffSync finds the smartest way to keep operations running.**

StaffSync is a full-stack workforce management platform designed to help **schools, colleges, and industries** manage staff availability, replacements, schedules, workload, and real-time workforce adjustments from a single platform.

The core idea is simple:

**When a faculty member or employee becomes unavailable, StaffSync identifies suitable replacements, sends requests, and updates the relevant dashboards in real time.**

---

## 🚀 Problem Statement

Organizations often face unexpected staff shortages because of:

- Faculty/employee absence
- Shift changes
- Emergency leave
- Schedule conflicts
- Unbalanced workload
- Lack of available replacement staff
- Manual communication between administrators and staff

In many organizations, these problems are handled through calls, WhatsApp messages, spreadsheets, or manual coordination.

This can lead to:

- Delayed replacements
- Schedule conflicts
- Uneven workload
- Missed lectures
- Staffing gaps
- Poor visibility for administrators

StaffSync aims to solve this problem through a centralized and intelligent workforce adjustment system.

---

# 💡 Our Solution

StaffSync provides a centralized platform where administrators can:

- Manage faculty and employees
- Create timetables and shifts
- Track availability and attendance
- Detect staffing gaps
- Find suitable replacements
- Send replacement/proxy requests
- Manage shift swaps
- Track workload and overtime
- Import staff and timetable data from PDFs
- Monitor the workforce in real time
- View organization-wide reports

Staff members get their own personalized dashboards based on their individual records.

# 🧠 Intelligent Workforce Adjustment

The key feature of StaffSync is its intelligent replacement system.

When a faculty member or employee becomes unavailable, StaffSync evaluates potential replacements based on multiple factors.

### Replacement Factors

- Availability
- Schedule conflicts
- Subject compatibility
- Skills
- Certifications
- Department
- Current workload
- Weekly working hours
- Overtime
- Rest requirements
- Fairness
- Shift eligibility

The system generates suitable candidates and provides a recommendation score along with the reason for the recommendation.
